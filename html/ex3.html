Traditional Gujarati Recipes

1. Khaman Dhokla

Overview: A soft, fluffy, and savory steamed snack made from gram flour (besan).

Main Ingredients: Gram flour, green chillies, ginger paste, turmeric, lemon juice, mustard seeds, and curry leaves.

Method: Prepare a smooth batter, steam it until spongy, and pour a tempering of mustard seeds, green chillies, and warm sugar-water over the top. Garnish with fresh coriander.

2. Undhiyu

Overview: A rich, mixed vegetable delicacy traditionally prepared during the winter season and Uttarayan festival.

Main Ingredients: Surti papdi, baby potatoes, small brinjals, purple yam (kand), and fried fenugreek dumplings (methi muthia).

Method: Stuff the vegetables with a flavorful green paste of coconut, peanuts, and green garlic, then slow-cook together until tender and fragrant.

3. Gujarati Dal

Overview: A mildly sweet and tangy lentil soup that forms the core of everyday Gujarati meals.

Main Ingredients: Toor dal (pigeon peas), jaggery, tomatoes, kokum or lemon juice, peanuts, and basic spices.

Method: Boil and mash the dal, simmer with jaggery and kokum to balance sweet and sour flavors, and finish with a fragrant tempering of cloves, cinnamon, and cumin.
