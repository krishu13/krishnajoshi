The Clockmaker and the Boy

Once upon a time, an old man named Jonathan lived in a small village. He was a master clockmaker who could fix any broken clock. People believed his clocks had special magic because they never stopped working.

The Broken Watch

One evening, a young boy named Leo came to his shop. He was holding a broken pocket watch and crying softly. "Sir, this was my grandfather's watch," Leo whispered. "It stopped working yesterday, and I miss him so much."

Jonathan smiled warmly and took the watch. He sat down at his wooden table, lit a small lamp, and began to work carefully. He cleaned the tiny gears, fixed the spring, and polished the glass. After an hour of quiet work, the watch began to tick again.

Leo smiled with joy and tried to pay him. Jonathan shook his head and said, "Keep your money, young man. Always remember that kindness is the best gift of all." Leo thanked him happily, and Jonathan felt glad in his heart.
