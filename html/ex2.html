About Bhavnagar City

Bhavnagar is a prominent coastal city located in the state of Gujarat, India. It was founded in 1723 AD by ruler Bhavsinhji Gohil and served as the capital of the princely state of Bhavnagar.

Key Highlights

The city is historically important as a major trading port in the Gulf of Khambhat. Today, it is widely recognized for its thriving industries, including salt production, ship recycling at nearby Alang, and diamond polishing.

Popular Attractions

Bhavnagar is home to several famous landmarks, including the beautiful hilltop marble temple Takhteshwar Temple, the scenic Gaurishankar Lake (Bor Talav), and the historic Nilambag Palace. It is also famous across India for its delicious traditional snack, Bhavnagari Gathiya.
