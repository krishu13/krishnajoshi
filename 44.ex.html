Bhavnagar

Bhavnagar is a historic city located in the Saurashtra region of Gujarat, India. Founded in 1724 by Bhavsinhji Gohil, it served as the capital of the Bhavnagar State before integrating into the Indian Union.

Tokyo

Tokyo is the capital city of Japan and one of the most populous metropolitan areas in the world. It is known for its incredible blend of deeply rooted traditional culture and cutting-edge modern technology.
